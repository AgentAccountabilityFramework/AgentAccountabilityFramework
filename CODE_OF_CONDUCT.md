# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds, experiences, and points of view. This code applies to project spaces and to conduct when representing the project.

## Our expectations

- Treat people with respect and consideration. Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking the person offering them.
- Welcome questions, different levels of experience, and constructive disagreement. Give and accept feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy and boundaries. Ask before sharing someone else's private information or contacting them privately about project matters.
- Avoid harassment, discriminatory or offensive remarks about identity or protected characteristics, threats, intimidation, sexual attention, spam, and disruptive behavior.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, listen when someone raises a concern, and adjust your behavior.

## Reporting a concern

Email conduct concerns to [admin@agentaccountabilityframework.org](mailto:admin@agentaccountabilityframework.org). Do not post the report or identifying details in a public issue. You may include links, dates, context, and how you can be reached; share only what you are comfortable providing.

Maintainers handling a report should limit access to those who need it, consider conflicts of interest, and protect the privacy and safety of those involved as far as possible. They may request more information and take proportionate action, including removing content, limiting participation, or excluding someone from project spaces. See [moderation guidance](maintenance/moderation.md). No particular response time or outcome is guaranteed.
