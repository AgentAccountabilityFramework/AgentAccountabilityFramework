# Terminology

[Framework contents](README.md) · [Previous: Scope](02-scope.md) · [Next: Framework Core](04-framework-core.md)

These short explanations introduce terms you will encounter in the framework. For the full definitions used to interpret the controls, read [Appendix A](appendices/a-terminology.md). Those definitions have normative effect: they fix what the terms mean when you apply a requirement ([Section 3](../source/v1.0/03-terminology.md#3-terminology), [Section 4](../source/v1.0/04-framework-core.md#4-the-framework-core-principles-and-controls)).

## Agent Accountability

The ability to establish, with evidence, what agent acted, on whose authority, within what bounds, and how it can be stopped. [Source](../source/v1.0/00-front-matter.md#abstract).

## Agent Accountability Framework

A framework of principles and testable controls for governing AI agents acting with delegated authority. [Source](../source/v1.0/00-front-matter.md#abstract).

## Principle

One of the framework's six top-level requirements. [Source](../source/v1.0/appendices/a-terminology.md#principle).

## Registry

The authoritative record of agent identities, sponsors, owning teams, lifecycle status, and authorized tools and models. [Source](../source/v1.0/appendices/a-terminology.md#registry).

## Credentials

Evidence an agent presents to authenticate as its identity, bound to that identity and the intended resource. [Source](../source/v1.0/appendices/a-terminology.md#credential).

## Identity

The unique, durable identifier of an agent in the registry, distinct from every human user and every other agent. [Source](../source/v1.0/appendices/a-terminology.md#agent-identity). [Principle](principles/01-identity.md).
