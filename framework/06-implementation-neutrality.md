# Implementation Neutrality

[Framework contents](README.md) · [Previous: Conformance and Assessment](05-conformance-and-assessment.md) · [Next: Relationship to Standards](07-relationship-to-standards.md)

Source: Section 6: Implementation Neutrality.

Your organization chooses how to meet the framework's requirements. Conformance cannot depend on a particular product, architecture, platform, protocol, or methodology, including one offered by the framework's steward.

Terms such as **registry**, **enforcement layer**, and **containment boundary** describe properties you must be able to demonstrate. They do not specify components you must buy.

For example, a registry must provide an authoritative record of the agents admitted to act and the information required by Control 1.2. You choose the technology that provides that record. The same principle applies to every control: choose the means, then show that the requirement is met.
