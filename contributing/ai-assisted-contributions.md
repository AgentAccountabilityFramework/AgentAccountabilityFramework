# AI-assisted contributions

AI tools can help with drafting, editing, research, or review. Contributors remain responsible for the material they submit and for responding to review.

Before submitting, read and verify the complete contribution yourself. Check claims against reliable sources, inspect links and citations, and compare framework statements with the relevant Markdown source. Do not submit fabricated citations, invented requirements, or text you cannot explain. Follow the [editorial guide](editorial-guide.md) and identify proposed changes to the framework as proposals.

Only provide material to a tool when you have permission to do so. Do not include private reports, personal information, confidential material, or other content you are not authorized to share. Ensure that you have permission to contribute the resulting material. If tool use or its limitations would help reviewers assess the contribution, describe them in the pull request.
