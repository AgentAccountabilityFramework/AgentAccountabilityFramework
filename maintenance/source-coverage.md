## Conversion review and departures

No substantive departures from the PDF were made. The source preserves all section and principle introductions, 45 complete controls, all 37 Appendix A definitions, the front matter, and the text in Sections 5 and 8 and Appendix B. Requirements remain normative; Rationale and Assessment remain informative, with the definitions and boundary rule retaining normative effect (Section 4).

Presentation changes are limited to Markdown headings and tables, joined wrapped lines, navigation, stable control anchors, linked cross-references, and provenance metadata. Appendix A's table becomes one heading per definition. The original duplicated period in Section 3 is retained. Original attribution and document metadata are preserved in the front matter.

The six derived principle pages contain concise introductions and all 45 complete controls, with source links; control wording is retained from the source. The [Purpose page](../framework/01-purpose.md) summarizes the purpose, audience, and intended outcomes from Section 1, including the organization's choice of implementation. The [Scope page](../framework/02-scope.md) covers the included agents and exclusions from Section 2 and explains the complete boundary rule in plain language, preserving its meaning and links to the source controls. The [Terminology page](../framework/03-terminology.md) defines the six selected terms mapped above. The [Framework Core page](../framework/04-framework-core.md) explains how to read and apply the controls from Section 4, with domain questions from the source principle introductions and scope-tag, action-class, and reach definitions from Appendix A. Sections 5–8 and both appendices cover the source material listed above. Section 7 adds verified primary links for related work. Appendix B draws its named credits from the source front matter. No assessment method, governance decision, or licensing terms have been invented. Proposed future departures must be recorded separately from transcription corrections.

The explanatory framework pages and principle introductions use the direct voice established in [Scope](../framework/02-scope.md): familiar verbs, concrete examples, and explanations of technical terms. The Purpose and Core revisions retain the meaning of Sections 1 and 4, including the distinction between requirements and guidance, the rule that every obligation in a Requirement must be met, scope-tag applicability, and the normative effect of definitions and the boundary rule. The principle summaries and the Identity example link to their source principles. Source text and control requirements are unchanged.

At transcription time, validation established that the rendered source text matched all 17,756 whitespace-separated tokens of the PDF extraction in order, including punctuation, after removing editorial aids and restoring field-label punctuation. All 45 controls retain their three fields in order and their scope tags. Local links, Markdown anchors, and PDF page fragments were checked across all changed and added files. The transcription and link checks were run directly for that conversion; the repository checker was empty at the time.

Repository citations use Markdown source files and section or control anchors. The authoring convention in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md#source-of-truth) applies to derived prose; the source transcription is unchanged.

Current automated validation uses [check-docs.py](../scripts/check-docs.py) to check local links and anchors and compare all complete control fields and formal definitions with the source, including linked references. Its [regression tests](../scripts/test_check_docs.py) introduce errors into temporary copies to verify detection. See the [checks guide](checks.md) for commands and limits. These checks supplement review of meaning in explanatory prose.
