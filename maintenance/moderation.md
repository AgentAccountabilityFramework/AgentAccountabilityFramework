# Moderation guidance

The [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) sets expectations for project spaces. This page offers practical guidance to maintainers; it does not assign moderator roles.

For ordinary issues and discussions, welcome participants, ask for missing context, keep discussion relevant, and explain when an issue is closed or redirected. Critique ideas without speculating about contributors' motives. Apply the same standards to maintainers and contributors.

For a conduct concern, avoid asking for sensitive details in public. If a private report is received, restrict access to people who need it and avoid involving anyone with a conflict of interest. Consider the available information and the safety of participants, document the decision privately, and choose a proportionate response. Actions can include a private request to stop, removal of content, limits on participation, or exclusion from project spaces. Share only the information needed to carry out a decision; do not promise confidentiality or a particular outcome.

Direct private conduct reports to [admin@agentaccountabilityframework.org](mailto:admin@agentaccountabilityframework.org). The same address is available for general contact with the AAF team.
