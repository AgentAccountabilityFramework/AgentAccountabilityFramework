# 5. Conformance and Assessment

[To be developed.] This section will define how conformance to the framework is assessed. Its positions, to be formalized before 1.0, are these. Conformance is stated for the estate and evidenced agent by agent: every agent in production is covered, and a finding against any agent is a finding against the estate unless it is recorded as a declared exception with a remediation window. Partial coverage of the population is the failure mode the framework exists to eliminate and is never conformance; an organization may, however, claim conformance to a declared subset of controls provided the subset covers every agent, so that adoption can be staged by control and not by population. Every parameter this document leaves to the organization (each defined window, cadence, period, threshold, and tolerance) is published in the organization’s conformance statement, and the framework sets floors only where a floor matters ([6.1](principles/06-recourse.md#control-6-1)). Assessment evidence shows each property enforced and maintained through change, not only declared; where an Assessment samples, the sample size, selection method, and confidence are stated, and a universal claim is evidenced by reconciliation against independent sources, not by the sample alone. Formal conformance keywords and assessment methodology will be defined here. Conformance under this framework asserts that the organization’s agents are accountable as this document defines the term. It does not assert that the organization is secure, or that the models in use are safe or aligned.
